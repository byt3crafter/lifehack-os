User Guide

Everything you need to know about LifeHack OS

What is LifeHack OS?
A personal operating system that tracks your habits, health, finances, journal, books, contacts, and more — all in one place. An AI assistant has access to all your data and can help you log meals, create habits, track spending, and give personalized advice. Everything is private and self-hosted.
Health Modules
✅ Habits
Track daily habits with strength scores, phases, micro-tasks, and scheduled times. Set a time like 08:00 and it shows overdue if you miss it. Log why you missed with reason tracking.
🍽 Food & Drinks
Log meals and drinks with AI photo analysis. Snap a photo and the AI identifies the food and estimates calories. Track protein, carbs, fat. Rate meals and log mood after eating. Multiple images per entry.
⏱ Fasting
Intermittent fasting timer with body stage tracking (Fed State → Early Fasting → Glycogen Depletion → Fat Burning → Autophagy). Live timer with progress ring, hydration tips per stage.
✨ Wellness
Track water intake (glasses per day with goal), sleep (bedtime, wake time, quality), and get a wellness score (0-100) calculated from habits + water + sleep + food.
Productivity Modules
💡 Deep Work
Start focused work sessions with a timer. Track by project. See daily, weekly, and total focus time. Integrates with Vikunja for task management.
🏆 Challenges
Set personal challenges (e.g., "Stop drinking for 90 days"). Track streaks, check-in daily, see heatmap calendar. If you break it, log why and restart.
📄 Notes
Quick capture ideas with title, body (markdown), tags, and folders. Pin important notes. Search across all notes. Random note button to rediscover old ideas.
Life Modules
💰 Finance
Connect Firefly III for live accounts and budgets. Track subscriptions with "worth it?" rating. Log income. Spending insights, recurring expense detection, anomaly alerts, weekly digest. AI can scan receipts.
📝 Journal
Daily entries with mood, energy, gratitude (3 things), daily wins, lessons learned, and freeform writing. Tags for organization. "On This Day" shows past entries. Full-text search.
📖 Books
Track books you're reading, want to read, and have finished. Log reading sessions (pages per sitting). Set a yearly reading challenge. Star ratings and reviews.
👤 Contacts CRM
Personal relationship manager. Add contacts with relationship type, reach-out frequency, birthday. Get reminders when you're overdue to contact someone. Log interactions (calls, texts, meetings). Gift ideas per person.
🎯 Discover
Bucket list of places, experiences, and skills you want to try. Track status (want → planning → done), add photos, rate experiences.
AI Assistant
🤖 Chat Mode
A full-screen AI chat that knows all your data. Just talk naturally:
• "Log 30 push-ups" — creates a habit entry
• "I had a black coffee" — logs a drink
• "How am I doing this week?" — analyzes your data
• "Add Atomic Habits to my reading list" — adds a book
• "I called my mom today" — logs a contact interaction
• Snap a photo of a receipt to auto-log expenses
• Snap a meal photo for AI nutrition analysis
Supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama (local), and more.
Sharing & API
• Invite friends & family — generate invite links from your Profile
• Each person gets their own data — complete isolation, nobody sees your stuff
• API keys — generate from Profile to use the REST API programmatically
• Export your data — Settings > About > Export (JSON per module or all)
• PWA — add to home screen on mobile for app-like experience
